# Audio Lab
The Audio Lab (`tools/audiolab`) is a small app for Windows and macOS for trying CarbonAudio changes without a game.
It shows a top-down 2D canvas where you place the listener, emitters playing events, Spatial Audio rooms and portals,
and walls that block sightlines, and edit each room's reverb aux bus, transmission loss, priority and room tone while
the Wwise Profiler is connected.

It runs CarbonAudio natively, without Python or exefile, through the C++ host API in
[`include/CarbonAudioHost.h`](../include/CarbonAudioHost.h): it boots Blue itself, starts CarbonAudio with
`CarbonAudioHost::Start`, creates `AudRoom`, `AudPortal` and `AudEmitter` through `BeClasses` and places them through
`ITr2BoxVolume` and `ITr2AudEmitter`, the same way trinity does.

Build it with the `CARBON_AUDIO_BUILD_TOOLS` option, which also installs its vcpkg dependencies (Dear ImGui and
nlohmann-json, the `tools` manifest feature):
```powershell
cmake --preset x64-windows-internal -DCARBON_AUDIO_BUILD_TOOLS=ON
cmake --build .cmake-build-x64-windows-internal --target AudioLab --config Internal
.cmake-build-x64-windows-internal\Internal\audiolab_internal.exe
```
In VS Code, the "Build Audio Lab" and "Launch Audio Lab" tasks do the same on Windows; Launch asks for the page to
open.

- It loads the SoundBanks of its own Wwise project, `tools/audiolab/Wwise/AudioLab`, from its output folder
  `tools/audiolab/Wwise/SoundBanks`, and reads the events from Wwise's own `SoundbanksInfo.json`.
  `Init.bnk` has three room reverb aux busses (`AudioLab_Hall`, `AudioLab_Room`, `AudioLab_Hangar`, RoomVerb) and
  `AudioLab.bnk` has `Play_AudioLab_Speech` (the event new emitters play), `Play_AudioLab_Loop`,
  `Play_AudioLab_SpeechPhrase` (a one-shot) and `Play_AudioLab_RoomTone`, whose sounds use game-defined aux sends and
  diffraction/transmission. New rooms get `AudioLab_Hall`, so walking in and out of them is audible.
- Use `--soundbanks <folder>` for another folder generated by Wwise and `--bank <name.bnk>` (repeatable) to load only
  some of its SoundBanks. A `SoundPrioritizationMetadata.json` in that folder, as the CCP post-generation step
  writes, adds what Wwise does not export, such as stopped-by events.
- When Wwise generates the SoundBanks again, the lab reloads them and keeps its scene.
- WASD walks the listener relative to where it faces (Shift: faster), Q/E turn it. The scene's tools (1 to 5) select
  and move things, draw rooms, place emitters, draw walls and place portals on a room's wall. Delete removes, right-drag
  pans and the wheel zooms. Objects are coloured by the room CarbonAudio placed them in.
- Pages (the toolbar's first combo, or `--page <page>`) each set up one feature with its own scene, controls and
  checks; the Sandbox is the free scene. `audiolab_internal --check all|<page>|<page>/<check>` runs checks without
  a window, and each page's checks are registered as the `audiolab_<page>` test.
- The culling of sound prioritization stays off in the lab, so every emitter is awake.
- To change the content, open `tools/audiolab/Wwise/AudioLab/AudioLab.wproj` in Wwise and generate the SoundBanks.
